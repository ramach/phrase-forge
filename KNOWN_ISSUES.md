# Known Issues

- SQLite leaderboard and feedback data are temporary on Streamlit Community Cloud and may reset after app restarts or redeploys.
- AI generation requires `OPENAI_API_KEY` and consumes API credits.
- AI suggestions are not trusted directly; they may be rejected if the deterministic engine finds no legal solution.
- The current phrase bank is an initial curated collection, not yet the planned thousands of audited puzzles.
