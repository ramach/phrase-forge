# Sprint 2.1.2 — Source Word Cards

Source words now show readable, high-contrast grammatical roles rather than muted captions. Each has an expandable mini Word Card with meanings, examples, usage and provenance where available. RAIN and DELAY have curated noun/verb classifications and examples. Other words use existing local lexicon metadata and unverified classifications are labeled suggestions. Missing content is not invented.
