# Manifesto Evolution

The **Product Manifesto should not be frozen after Version 1.0.**

Unlike implementation specifications, which naturally evolve as features are added and refined, the Manifesto serves a different purpose. It captures the enduring philosophy of Phrase Forge—why the product exists, what it stands for, and the principles that guide every decision.

These principles should remain stable, but they should not become static.

As Phrase Forge grows through player feedback, educational research, and community participation, the Manifesto should mature alongside it. New insights may strengthen existing principles, clarify the product's purpose, or introduce ideas that better reflect how players experience the platform.

The Manifesto should therefore be treated as a **living document**. It should evolve deliberately and infrequently, only when the product's understanding of its mission deepens.

Every revision should continue to reinforce the core promise of Phrase Forge:

- Play should inspire curiosity.
- Exploration should encourage discovery.
- Learning should emerge naturally through participation.
- Every puzzle should provide an opportunity to discover a new word, a new grammatical role, a new usage, or a deeper nuance of language.
- Players should not only learn from the product—they should gradually help shape it by contributing themes, examples, relationships, and ideas that enrich the experience for the entire community.

Ultimately, the Manifesto should remain the product's moral and philosophical compass. It exists not to describe what Phrase Forge currently does, but to remind every contributor what Phrase Forge aspires to become.
